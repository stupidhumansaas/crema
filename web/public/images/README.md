# Coffee photography

These editorial photographs are stored locally so the page does not depend on a third-party image request. They illustrate brewing and sharing coffee; they do not represent a particular listed roaster or product.

- `coffee-craft.jpg`: pour-over brewing, [Unsplash source image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1442512595331-e89e73853f31). Downloaded at 1,000 pixels wide.
- `coffee-ritual.jpg`: three people sharing coffee, [Unsplash source image](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1495474472287-4d71bcdd2085). Downloaded at 1,400 pixels wide.

The responsive crops are controlled by `app/editorial.css`. Roaster logos remain in `data/logos/` and are copied into `public/logos/` by the existing prebuild script.
