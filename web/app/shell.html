How Crema works

Our methodology and standards

Crema helps you find coffee from independent UK roasters. Here is where our information comes from, how we make recommendations, and how to tell us when something is wrong.

This is a private preview. Crema is still in development and shared for feedback only. Roaster names, logos and bag images belong to the roasters and are shown here to identify them while we build; they haven't yet given permission for a public launch.
Our promise: our search ranking and taste-matching are never for sale — they're decided on quality and taste, never on who pays. Roasters can pay for a richer profile and for visibility, and anything paid to place is labelled "Featured" and kept out of the matching that decides what we suggest. How we make money ↗

Complete, and curated

Crema sets out to cover the UK's independent speciality roasters — the ones worth knowing — so you can find them in one place rather than hunting across a dozen sites. Coverage is only half the job, though. A plain list of hundreds of roasters leaves you to do all the work; we don't.

On top of the directory, we curate what we put in front of you. The home page, our recommendations and our features highlight the coffees and roasters we rate most highly, so the best rises to the top — and the full list is still there when you want to dig. Our view is that quality and focus tend to go together, so a roaster with a considered, well-kept range usually rewards you more than one roasting everything at once. That shapes what we recommend first, not who we're willing to list.

Where our information comes from

  • Roasters. We keep a list of UK roasters — aiming to cover every independent speciality roaster worth knowing — and work through them one by one. Each has a status: fully listed, in progress, or not started yet.
  • Coffees. Origin, process, roast, tasting notes and price come from the roaster's own product page, recorded as listed, with the date we checked. If a roaster doesn't say something, such as the roast level, we show "Not stated" rather than guess.
  • What roasters offer. Subscriptions, wholesale, cafés, B Corp and the rest are only marked "yes" when the roaster's own website or certifying body shows it, and we record the date checked. Decaf, single origins, light roasts and pods are worked out from the coffees we list, and the roaster's page says so.
  • Supermarket coffees. Taken from supermarket listings and each brand's own pack description, including its strength rating. Prices are typical shelf prices on the day we checked.
  • Grinder settings. From each manufacturer's own grind chart. Where a manufacturer publishes none, we use independent grind-size measurements and say so.

How we recommend coffees

"You might also like" compares coffees on:

  • shared tasting notes, and notes from the same flavour family (chocolate, citrus, berries and so on)
  • overall taste group and how close the roast levels are
  • the same process or origin
  • for pods, the same machine
  • price per cup, so suggestions stay in a similar price range

We show no more than two coffees from any one roaster, so you discover new ones. Coffees without tasting notes aren't recommended, because we can't match them fairly. A roaster's plan plays no part in any of this — paid or free, every roaster is matched to you on taste and quality alone. Your likes, equipment and grinder are saved in your own browser, and with your profile if you choose to save one.

Prices

We show the price the roaster lists. "From" means it's the smallest of several bag sizes. Price per cup assumes 15g of coffee per cup, or one pod, so a 1kg bag and a 150g bag can be compared fairly. Where the bag size isn't confirmed, the coffee's page says so.

Links, logos and names

Links to a roaster go straight to the roaster and earn us nothing — they carry a referral tag only so we can count the customers we send them. Some equipment links are affiliate links that earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you; those are marked, and a commission never decides what we feature. See how we make money. Roaster names and logos belong to the roasters and are shown only to identify them; any roaster can ask us to change or remove them.

Corrections

Coffee changes fast and we will sometimes get things wrong. If you spot a mistake, or you're a roaster and something about you is out of date, tell us and we'll fix it and note the date it was updated. Contact details will be added before launch.

Transparency

How we make money

Crema is free to browse and always will be. We're funded by roasters, who pay for a listing, and by a small commission on coffee equipment — never by taking a cut of what a roaster sells.

In plain terms. Roasters pay us; readers never do, and we take no cut of a roaster's sales. Paying buys a richer profile and more visibility — clearly labelled — never a better position in search or in the coffees we match to your taste.
Discovery is never for sale. Search and taste-matching use quality only, so a free roaster with the best coffee for you can still be your top result.
