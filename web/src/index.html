How Crema works

Our methodology and standards

Crema helps you find coffee from independent UK roasters. Here is where our information comes from, how we make recommendations, and how to tell us when something is wrong.

This is a private preview. Crema is still in development and shared for feedback only. Roaster names, logos and bag images belong to the roasters and are shown here to identify them while we build; they haven't yet given permission for a public launch.
Our promise: recommendations and rankings are never for sale. If we ever show a paid placement, it will be labelled "Sponsored" and kept out of the matching that decides what we suggest to you.

Where our information comes from

  • Roasters. We keep a list of UK roasters and work through them one by one. Each has a status: fully listed, in progress, or not started yet.
  • Coffees. Origin, process, roast, tasting notes and price come from the roaster's own product page, recorded as listed, with the date we checked. If a roaster doesn't say something, such as the roast level, we show "Not stated" rather than guess.
  • What roasters offer. Subscriptions, wholesale, cafés, B Corp and the rest are only marked "yes" when the roaster's own website or certifying body shows it, and we record the date checked. Decaf, single origins, light roasts and pods are worked out from the coffees we list, and the roaster's page says so.
  • Supermarket coffees. Taken from supermarket listings and each brand's own pack description, including its strength rating. Prices are typical shelf prices on the day we checked.
  • Grinder settings. From each manufacturer's own grind chart. Where a manufacturer publishes none, we use independent grind-size measurements and say so.

How we recommend coffees

"You might also like" compares coffees on:

  • shared tasting notes, and notes from the same flavour family (chocolate, citrus, berries and so on)
  • overall taste group and how close the roast levels are
  • the same process or origin
  • for pods, the same machine
  • price per cup, so suggestions stay in a similar price range

We show no more than two coffees from any one roaster, so you discover new ones. Coffees without tasting notes aren't recommended, because we can't match them fairly. Your likes, equipment and grinder are saved only in your own browser.

Prices

We show the price the roaster lists. "From" means it's the smallest of several bag sizes. Price per cup assumes 15g of coffee per cup, or one pod, so a 1kg bag and a 150g bag can be compared fairly. Where the bag size isn't confirmed, the coffee's page says so.

Links, logos and names

Shop links currently earn us nothing. If that changes, every paid link will be clearly marked. Roaster names and logos belong to the roasters and are shown only to identify them; any roaster can ask us to change or remove them.

Corrections

Coffee changes fast and we will sometimes get things wrong. If you spot a mistake, or you're a roaster and something about you is out of date, tell us and we'll fix it and note the date it was updated. Contact details will be added before launch.
